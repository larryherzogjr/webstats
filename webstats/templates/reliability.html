Latency is nginx's total $request_time, measured from reading the first request byte through sending the response. Performance includes successful human, non-asset GET requests and excludes recognized probe paths. A latency regression requires at least three timed samples in each equal window, a 50% rise, and 100 ms of absolute movement. Payload anomalies require three responses in each window, 50% movement, and 25 KB. Application errors exclude recognized probe paths; scanner noise is reported separately. Historic latency begins when timed logging is deployed, while retained response sizes are rebuilt during migration.
