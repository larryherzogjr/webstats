Reconstructed episodes

Human page requests only

An episode begins when a site's human page traffic clears an adaptive threshold derived from the preceding 28 days: at least five requests, twice baseline, two standard deviations above baseline, and three requests above baseline. Nearby trigger days are joined, the decay is followed for up to seven days, and the following week is compared with the old baseline. Driver attribution uses permanent daily rollups; it is descriptive, not a claim of causation. Privacy-protected sites are excluded.
