    The Chronicle takes bounded snapshots of public sitemap and robots policy state. It stores compact changes, not full sitemap copies. When a page leaves a sitemap, Webstats probes at most 64 removed URLs per site and scan to distinguish a redirect, a 404/410 disappearance, and an unlisted but reachable page. Network failures never mark every page removed. Crawler reactions use permanent daily rollups; page-level reactions remain hidden for privacy-protected sites.
